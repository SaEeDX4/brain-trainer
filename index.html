How Challenge Mode Works:

Time Attack Challenge

In Time Attack mode, you'll answer 20 math questions as quickly as possible:

  • Unlike regular mode, the timer counts up instead of down
  • Your goal is to complete all questions in the shortest possible time
  • Your best time is saved and displayed for future attempts
  • Perfect accuracy is required to set a new record

Tips for Success

  • Practice in regular mode first to improve your skills
  • Focus on accuracy - wrong answers don't improve your time
  • Use keyboard shortcuts for faster input
  • Try to beat your previous best time
